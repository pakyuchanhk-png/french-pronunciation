How the French flashcard maker works

Paste a list of French words (or upload a .csv), one per line, and press Create flashcards. Each word becomes a card you can flip to see its English meaning, hear pronounced aloud, and swipe through like a deck. Switch to Spelling mode to test whether you can write each word from its meaning. Everything runs in your browser and your list is kept only on your device.

About the translations

English meanings are filled in automatically using a free translation service, so treat them as a helpful guide rather than a dictionary — machine translation can miss context, and grammatical gender (le/la) is estimated from the word ending. For exact meanings, you can type your own English after each word (for example canard, duck).

Frequently asked questions

Is my word list uploaded anywhere?

No. Your list is processed in your own browser and stored locally on your device. Only the individual words are sent to the translation service to fetch a meaning.

How many words can I add?

You can add hundreds at once. Very large lists take longer because each word is translated individually, and the free translation service has daily limits — if translations stop, try again later or add your own English.

Can I practise spelling?

Yes. Switch to Spelling mode and type the French word from its English meaning (you can also hear it first). We tell you if you’re correct, and flag when only the accents are off.

Why is the le/la sometimes wrong?

Gender is guessed from the word’s ending, which is right most of the time but not always. Include the article yourself (e.g. la table) for accuracy.
